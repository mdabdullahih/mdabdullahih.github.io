Research & Publications

Conference research, applied machine learning, cybersecurity, and collaborative academic work.

Conference Paper · Cybersecurity

Security Awareness, Technical Confidence, and Protective Behavior in Residential Wi-Fi and IoT Environments: An Empirical Study in Bangladesh

International Conference on Knowledge Integration and Educational Leadership (ICKIEL 2026)
Presented October 17–19, 2026 · AUST, Dhaka, Bangladesh
Publication date: October 20, 2026

An empirical study examining security awareness, technical confidence, and protective behavior among residential Wi-Fi and IoT users in Bangladesh. The study analyzes 326 responses and investigates 15 multivariate relationships using Pearson's Chi-square test at a 0.05 significance level.

The findings examine residential security practices involving administrator credentials, firmware updates, password complexity, guest networks, IoT credentials, Wi-Fi security protocols, and WPS configuration.

Authors: Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun and Prof. Dr. A. H. M. Saifullah Sadi.

Accepted & Presented
Conference Paper · Deep Learning

Hybrid Deep Networks with Layer-Wise Tail Optimization and Explainability for Mango Leaf Disease Classification

IEEE 3rd International Conference on Computing, Applications and Systems (COMPAS 2026)
October 9–10, 2026 · University of Dhaka, Bangladesh
Paper ID: 39

Presents MangoLeafNet, a hybrid deep learning approach combining gated MobileNetV2 and DenseNet201 with Grad-CAM-based explainability for mango leaf disease classification.

The study uses 2,485 images augmented to 12,000 and reports 99.44% accuracy on the self-collected dataset and 91.70% on the external MangoLeafBD dataset.

Authors: Raisa Ferdush, Md. Rakibul Islam Raj, Sanjida Rahman, Sanzida Islam Smriti, Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun, and Md. Saymon Ahammad.

Accepted & Presented
Conference Paper · Medical Image Analysis

Deep Learning-Based Classification of Breast Cancer in Mammogram Images

International Conference on Data Analytics and Applications (IDAA 2025)
December 12–13, 2025 · Daffodil International University
Paper ID: 10285

Presents BreastNet, a deep learning-based approach for breast cancer classification using mammogram images. The research reports 99.50% accuracy on the Mendeley dataset and 95.55% on the INbreast dataset.

Authors: Raisa Ferdush, Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun, Mir Mynul Ahasan Mim, Md. Mustak Ahmed, Tahajib Jakir Khan, and Md. Saymon Ahammad.
