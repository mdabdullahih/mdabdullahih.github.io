Accepted & Presented · ICKIEL 2026

Security Awareness, Technical Confidence, and Protective Behavior in Residential Wi-Fi and IoT Environments: An Empirical Study in Bangladesh

International Conference on Knowledge Integration and Educational Leadership (ICKIEL 2026) October 20, 2026 AUST, Dhaka, Bangladesh

This empirical study investigates the relationships between user characteristics, technical confidence, residential network conditions, and protective security behaviors in residential Wi-Fi and IoT environments in Bangladesh.

Based on a quantitative cross-sectional survey of 326 respondents, the study examines 15 multivariate relationships using Pearson's chi-square tests at a significance level of α = 0.05. The findings identify significant associations involving administrative credential management, firmware updates, password complexity, guest-network usage, IoT credential management, Wi-Fi protocol selection, and WPS configuration.

The research provides empirical insights into the socio-technical dimensions of residential cybersecurity and highlights the need for practical, user-oriented security awareness and protection.

Authors: Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun and Prof. Dr. A. H. M. Saifullah Sadi.
Cybersecurity Wi-Fi Security IoT Security Statistical Analysis Security Awareness
Accepted & Presented · IEEE COMPAS 2026

Hybrid Deep Networks with Layer-Wise Tail Optimization and Explainability for Mango Leaf Disease Classification

IEEE 3rd International Conference on Computing, Applications and Systems (COMPAS 2026) October 9–10, 2026 University of Dhaka, Bangladesh

This research proposes MangoLeafNet, a hybrid deep learning architecture combining gated MobileNetV2 and DenseNet201 components for mango leaf disease classification, with Grad-CAM-based explainability.

The model was evaluated using a self-collected dataset of 2,485 images, expanded to 12,000 images through augmentation, and the external MangoLeafBD dataset to assess generalization performance.

MangoLeafNet achieved 99.44% classification accuracy on the self-collected dataset and 91.70% on the external MangoLeafBD dataset.

Authors: Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun, Raisa Ferdush, Md. Rakibul Islam Raj, Sanjida Rahman, Sanzida Islam Smriti, and Md. Saymon Ahammad.
Deep Learning MobileNetV2 DenseNet201 Grad-CAM Image Classification
Accepted & Presented · IDAA 2025

Deep Learning-Based Classification of Breast Cancer in Mammogram Images

International Conference on Data Analytics and Applications (IDAA 2025) December 12–13, 2025 Daffodil International University, Dhaka, Bangladesh Paper ID: 10285

This research explores BreastNet, a hybrid deep learning approach for breast cancer classification using mammogram images. The model was evaluated on the Mendeley and INbreast datasets to examine classification performance and generalization capability.

BreastNet achieved 99.50% classification accuracy on the Mendeley dataset and 95.55% on the external INbreast dataset.

Authors: Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun, Raisa Ferdush, Mir Mynul Ahasan Mim, Md. Mustak Ahmed, Tahajib Jakir Khan, and Md. Saymon Ahammad.
Medical Image Analysis Breast Cancer Classification Deep Learning Mammogram Computer Vision

Security Awareness and Technical Controls in Residential Wireless and IoT Environments: Empirical Analysis, Predictive Modeling, and Mitigation

M.Sc. in Software Engineering (Major in Cyber Security) Daffodil International University

My master's thesis investigates residential wireless and IoT security in Bangladesh, focusing on user awareness, technical confidence, protective practices, residential network conditions, and reported security incidents.
