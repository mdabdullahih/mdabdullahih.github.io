Security Awareness, Technical Confidence, and Protective Behavior in Residential Wi-Fi and IoT Environments: An Empirical Study in Bangladesh

International Conference on Knowledge Integration and Educational Leadership (ICKIEL 2026)
Accepted and Presented

Presented at the International Conference on Knowledge Integration and Educational Leadership (ICKIEL 2026), held on October 17–19, 2026, at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), Dhaka, Bangladesh.

This study empirically investigates the associations between user characteristics, technical confidence, residential network conditions, and protective security behaviors in Bangladesh’s residential Wi-Fi and IoT environments. Using a quantitative cross-sectional survey of 326 respondents (322 valid records), the study evaluates 15 multivariate relationships through Pearson’s chi-square tests (α = 0.05).

The findings identify significant associations across key practices, including administrative credential management, firmware updates, password complexity, guest-network usage, IoT credential management, Wi-Fi protocol selection, WPS configuration, and reported security incidents. In contrast, IoT micro-segmentation showed no statistically significant association with device volume and technical confidence. The study provides empirical insights into the socio-technical dimensions of residential cybersecurity and highlights the need for practical, user-oriented security guidance.

Authors: Md. Abdullah Ibna Harun and Prof. Dr. A. H. M. Saifullah Sadi
Cybersecurity Residential Wi-Fi IoT Security Security Awareness Statistical Analysis

Hybrid Deep Networks with Layer-Wise Tail Optimization and Explainability for Mango Leaf Disease Classification

IEEE 3rd International Conference on Computing, Applications and Systems (COMPAS 2026)
Accepted and Presented

Presented at the IEEE 3rd International Conference on Computing, Applications and Systems (COMPAS 2026), held on October 9–10, 2026, at the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Proposed MangoLeafNet, a gated hybrid deep learning architecture combining MobileNetV2 and DenseNet201 with Grad-CAM explainability to classify eight mango leaf disease categories. The study uses a self-collected dataset of 2,485 images from Bangladesh, expanded to 12,000 images through preprocessing and augmentation.

The proposed model achieved 99.44% accuracy on the self-collected dataset and 91.70% accuracy on the external MangoLeafBD dataset.

Co-authors: Raisa Ferdush, Md. Rakibul Islam Raj, Sanjida Rahman, Sanzida Islam Smriti, and Md. Saymon Ahammad.
Deep Learning Computer Vision MobileNetV2 DenseNet201 Grad-CAM

Deep Learning-Based Classification of Breast Cancer in Mammogram Images

International Conference on Intelligent Data Analysis and Applications (IDAA 2025)
Accepted and Presented

Accepted and presented at the International Conference on Intelligent Data Analysis and Applications (IDAA 2025), held on December 12–13, 2025, at Daffodil International University, Dhaka, Bangladesh (Paper ID: 10285).

Proposed BreastNet, a hybrid deep learning model for binary classification of mammogram images into benign and malignant categories. The model achieved 99.50% accuracy on the Mendeley dataset and 95.55% overall accuracy on the external INbreast dataset, demonstrating strong classification performance and generalization capability.

Co-authors: Raisa Ferdush, Mir Mynul Ahasan Mim, Md. Mustak Ahmed, Tahajib Jakir Khan, and Md. Saymon Ahammad.
Deep Learning Medical Image Analysis Breast Cancer Classification Computer Vision
